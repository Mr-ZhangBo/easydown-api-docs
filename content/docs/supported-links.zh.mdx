每个平台页面都包含已支持链接、全量响应、平台限制、错误处理和实时请求模式。

## [TikTok](/zh/api/tiktok)

- `tiktok.com/@{user}/video/{id}`
- `tiktok.com/@{user}/photo/{id}`
- `tiktok.com/t/{code}`

### 可用示例

- `https://www.tiktok.com/@jesscreatescontent/video/7647557116596718861`
- `https://www.tiktok.com/@creator/photo/7512345678901234567`
- `https://www.tiktok.com/t/ZP8pT81pD/`

## [抖音（Douyin）](/zh/api/douyin)

- `douyin.com/video/{id}`
- `douyin.com/note/{id}`
- `douyin.com/shipin/{id}`
- `v.douyin.com/{code}`

### 可用示例

- `https://v.douyin.com/f-QpBB_oD9Y/`
- `https://www.douyin.com/video/7641191614632071546`
- `https://www.douyin.com/note/7643104807914401929`

## [YouTube](/zh/api/youtube)

- `youtube.com/watch?v={id}`

- `youtube.com/live/{id}`

## [Threads](/zh/api/threads)

- `threads.com/@{user}/post/{id}`
- `threads.net/@{user}/post/{id}`
- `threads.com/@{user}/post/{id}/media`
- `threads.net/@{user}/post/{id}/media`
- `threads.com/share/{code}`
- `threads.net/share/{code}`
- `Supported post and share links may include query parameters`

### 可用示例

- `https://www.threads.com/@eekeai/post/DDhV3LWhrlk`
- `https://www.threads.com/share/_5O7Rn7A0/`

## [Bilibili / B站（仅大陆站）](/zh/api/bilibili)

- `bilibili.com/video/BV{id}`
- `bilibili.com/video/av{id}`
- `b23.tv/{code}`
- `bilibili.com/bangumi/play/{id}`
- `bilibili.com/cheese/play/{id}`

### 可用示例

- `https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1sW4y197cE/`
- `https://www.bilibili.com/video/av45306969/`
- `https://b23.tv/INJgdai`

> Bilibili 仅支持大陆站链接。bilibili.tv 与 bili.im（Bstation / 国际版）目前不支持。
